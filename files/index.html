About

Music that
carries stories.

I'm Josia Meyer — composer, music producer and sound designer based in Darmstadt. I started playing piano, drums, guitar and organ early on, and developed an understanding of music, emotion and structure that still drives everything I do today.

I studied Sound & Music Production at h-da Darmstadt, with a focus on music production, sound design and game audio. I then completed a 6-month internship at BOOM Library, contributing to professional sound libraries with field recording and sound design. Alongside studio work, I play keys in an indie-pop band from Darmstadt.
